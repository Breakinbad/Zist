Questions

Frequently asked

What does ZIST actually do?

ZIST gives you short, evidence-informed health assessments across areas like sleep, stress, movement, and energy. It then connects the dots across those areas to find what matters most for you, and gives you a personalized plan to track progress over time.

How long does an assessment take?

Each assessment takes a few minutes. You can complete them over a few days at your own pace — no need to do everything at once.

Is my data private?

We collect your email to send your assessment and occasional product updates — nothing more. We do not sell your email address. If ZIST collects health-assessment information in the future, we'll explain exactly how that data is used and protected before you submit it.

Is ZIST a medical device?

No. ZIST is a wellness tool designed to help you understand and track your health. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.

How much does it cost?

ZIST is free for early users. Sign up to get your first assessment at no cost.
